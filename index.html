Who we are

A movement built by young people, for young people

We believe young people are not simply the leaders of tomorrow—they are innovators, entrepreneurs, volunteers, problem-solvers, and community builders today.

Welcome to Alpha Youth Group, a youth empowerment movement based in Voi, Taita Taveta County, Kenya, with a vision to create meaningful opportunities for young people and contribute to the social and economic development of our communities.

Alpha Youth Group exists to bring young people together around a shared purpose: creating opportunities, developing potential, strengthening communities, and building a future in which every young person has the chance to contribute and grow.

Our ambition goes beyond building a membership organisation. We envision Alpha becoming a trusted platform where young people can connect, exchange ideas, develop skills, organise community initiatives, and work together to solve challenges affecting their generation.

Our home is Voi. Our roots are in Taita Taveta. Our vision reaches across the Coast Region and, ultimately, throughout Kenya.

Vision & Mission

Where we are going, and how we get there

Our Vision

Connecting, empowering and inspiring young people across Kenya.

To become a recognised, trusted, and impactful youth movement that connects, empowers, and inspires young people across Kenya to create sustainable opportunities and contribute to national development.

We envision a future where young people from different communities, backgrounds, and professions can come together through Alpha to learn from one another, build meaningful relationships, develop solutions, and create lasting change.

We want Alpha to stand for more than membership. We want it to represent opportunity, responsibility, innovation, unity, and hope.

Our Mission

Empowering young people with practical, lasting capability.

Our mission is to empower young people through financial literacy, entrepreneurship, education, leadership development, community service, innovation, and member welfare initiatives.

We aim to create a supportive environment where members can discover their strengths, share their ideas, develop practical skills, and participate in initiatives that improve their lives and communities.

Through responsible leadership, partnerships, volunteerism, and transparent management, we intend to build an organisation that can grow sustainably and serve its members with integrity.

Why Alpha matters to the Coast Region

Talent is everywhere. Access is not.

The Coast Region has young people with talent, creativity, ambition, and ideas capable of creating positive change. However, opportunities to connect, learn, collaborate, and develop those ideas may not always be easy to access.

Alpha Youth Group wants to help bridge that gap.

Beginning in Voi, we aim to build a strong foundation in Taita Taveta and gradually develop connections with young people in other parts of the Coast Region as our capacity grows. Our long-term ambition is to establish connections with young people throughout Kenya, creating a network through which members can share knowledge, identify opportunities, support community initiatives, and collaborate across counties.

When young people work together, local ideas can grow into regional opportunities, and regional cooperation can contribute to national development.

Our Dream

Every great movement begins with people who believe in a common vision.

Alpha Youth Group aims to build a community of 500 members and continue growing from there. We want each member to be more than a name on a registration list. We want members to have opportunities to participate, contribute ideas, learn, volunteer, and help shape the organisation's future.

Imagine a network of young people working together on community projects, developing business ideas, learning financial skills, supporting vulnerable members, and building relationships that open doors to new possibilities.

500 members can be the beginning. A connected generation can be the legacy.

The future we want to build

Eight pillars of the Alpha movement

01

Alpha Enterprise & Entrepreneurship

Encouraging entrepreneurship, business education, innovation, and responsible financial planning.

02

Alpha Skills & Digital Empowerment

Training and learning opportunities in digital skills, technology, communication, leadership, and other practical areas.

03

Alpha Community Development

Community initiatives such as environmental conservation, tree planting, clean-up campaigns, and education support.

04

Alpha Connect Kenya

Connecting young people from different counties through networking events, youth forums, mentorship, and collaborative projects.

05

Alpha Care & Member Welfare

A responsible welfare initiative helping eligible members facing approved emergencies—subject to clear rules and accountability.

06

Alpha Leadership Academy

Developing responsible young leaders through mentorship, public speaking, teamwork, and ethical leadership.

07

Alpha Opportunities Network

Building relationships with educational institutions, businesses, and employers to explore opportunities for training and mentorship.

08

Alpha Annual Youth Forum

A platform for young people to meet, discuss challenges, share ideas, showcase projects, and celebrate achievements.

Our commitment to the future

Words are not enough. We intend to show our work.

We recognise that a meaningful future cannot be built through words alone. It requires planning, committed leadership, active members, sound financial management, responsible partnerships, and projects that respond to real community needs.

We want our achievements to speak for us. We want our members to take pride in their contribution. We want our communities to see the value of young people working together for a common purpose.

Our journey begins in Voi, but our aspirations are not limited by geography.
